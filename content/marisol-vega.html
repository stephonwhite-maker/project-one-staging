About

Meet Marisol

Marisol trained in curl-specific cutting after years of watching curly clients leave salons with hair that only looked right wet. Every cut starts dry, curl by curl, so you see the shape as it is being made.

Her color work leans warm: copper, honey and soft dimension that stays kind to your curl pattern. Expect a long first visit, a lot of questions and a routine you can actually keep at home.
