Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site and visit the building. Here is what we do and how to reach us if something gets in your way.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across this site.

What we have done

Text and controls meet AA contrast. Every page works with a keyboard, with visible focus. Photos have text descriptions, and decorative textures are hidden from screen readers.

The accessibility panel in the corner lets you turn on larger text, high contrast, underlined links and readable spacing, and pause all motion. If your device asks for reduced motion, we turn the animations off for you.

Known limits

The map on the Location page is a placeholder for now. The address, tour hours and parking details are written out below it.

The floor plan is an image. The download and the image description list every room.

Visiting the building

If you have questions about getting into the building or moving around it, call us before your tour and we will walk you through it.

Tell us

If something on this site does not work for you, email hello@projectonesalonsuites.com or call (314) 555-0142. We reply within two business days.
