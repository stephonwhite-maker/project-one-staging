FAQ

Good questions

Still wondering about something? Ask the chat in the corner or call (314) 555-0142.

How long is a lease?

Leases run 6 or 12 months. After that you can renew month to month.

What does rent include?

Utilities, Wi-Fi, laundry, weekly cleaning of the common areas and snacks in the lounge.

How do I get into the building?

Keyless entry. The front door has an intercom, and every suite has a smart lock with your own code.

Can I bring my own products and equipment?

Yes. Bring your own chair, tools and product line. Every suite has a sink, outlets for two stations and locked storage.

What licenses do I need?

A current Missouri cosmetology, barber or esthetics license, plus liability insurance. We ask for both before move in.
