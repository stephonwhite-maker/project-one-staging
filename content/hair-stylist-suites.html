FAQ

Good questions

Is there a shampoo bowl in my suite?

Every suite has a sink for color mixing and rinsing. Two full shampoo bowls sit in the shared shampoo room down the hall.

Can I do color in an interior suite?

Yes. Interior suites have daylight LED panels, and most of our colorists choose them for the steady light.

Can I bring an assistant?

Yes. Every suite has outlets for two stations, and the studio suite fits a second chair.
