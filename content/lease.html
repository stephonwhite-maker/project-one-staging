FAQ

Leasing questions

Can I share a suite?

Yes. Two stylists can share a studio suite on one lease, and each gets their own code.

Do you take a commission?

No. You pay rent and keep everything you charge, including retail.

Can I sell my own retail products?

Yes. Your suite, your shelf, your product line.

What happens at the end of my lease?

Renew for another term or go month to month. Give us 30 days notice if you are moving on.
