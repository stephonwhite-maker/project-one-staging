(03) The space

You run your business. We run the building.

A restored 1920s storefront in The Grove, with tall windows, quiet halls and coffee on by 9. Walk in on a Tuesday morning and you will hear blow dryers, catch up in the lounge and get straight to work.

  • Keyless front entry with intercom
