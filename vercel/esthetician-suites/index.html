FAQ

Good questions

Will a lash or facial bed fit?

Yes. The 180 square foot studio suite fits a full treatment bed with room on both sides. Interior suites fit a compact bed.

Can I control the lighting?

Yes. Suites have their own switches, and you are welcome to bring a lamp or ring light.

What license do I need?

A current Missouri esthetics or cosmetology license, plus liability insurance before move in.
